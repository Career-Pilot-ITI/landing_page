Billing & Monetization FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do virtual coins work alongside subscriptions?+

Each monthly plan includes a recurring monthly coin allowance. Light features (like browsing challenges or reviewing reports) cost 0 coins. Heavy compute tasks (like full voice interviews or asynchronous CV optimization) consume coins. You can top up coins at any time without changing your plan.

Can Free users enter interview challenges?+

Yes! Free users can browse public challenges, join private challenges via codes, and submit interview attempts. Creating and hosting challenges is a Max plan entitlement.

What payment methods are supported?+

Career Pilot integrates with PayMob payment gateway, supporting local Credit/Debit cards, Meeza cards, and Egyptian Mobile Wallets (Vodafone Cash, Orange Money, etc.).

Do unused coins roll over?+

Purchased top-up coins never expire. Monthly subscription allowance coins refresh with each billing cycle.
