The Engineers Behind Career Pilot

Engineered by a collaborative multi-disciplinary team across Native Android, Native iOS, Cloud Software Engineering, and Quality Assurance under expert ITI mentorship.

The Vision

Bridging the gap between preparation and hiring reality.

Career Pilot was born out of a real engineering challenge: traditional job seekers practice with fragmented tools, receive hallucinatory advice from generic chatbots, and enter high-stakes interviews without objective delivery feedback. Our mission is to provide an evidence-grounded, multimodal intelligence platform that delivers measurable employability progress.
