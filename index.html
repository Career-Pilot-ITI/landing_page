Why Career Pilot

Career preparation breaks when context is fragmented.

CV editors, mock interview bots, ATS checkers, and delivery feedback usually live in disconnected silos. Career Pilot links the candidate, target role, and real-time performance memory.

Fragmented Tools

Your CV, target role requirements, and practice history live in disconnected apps with no shared context.

ATS Blind Spots

Strong engineers miss first-round screening because their CV fails to communicate required semantic evidence.

Generic AI Hallucinations

Ungrounded chatbots ask irrelevant questions and offer uncalibrated advice disconnected from real hiring rubrics.

Missing Delivery Feedback

Text-only practice misses eye contact, body posture stability, speech pacing (WPM), and communication flow.

Our Mission

Turn preparation into a measurable coaching journey.

We provide a closed-loop system: from CV ingestion and semantic gap analysis to adaptive mock interviews, live on-device delivery cues, and structured STAR feedback scoring.

Quick Answers

What visitors usually ask.

What is Career Pilot?+

Career Pilot is an end-to-end AI career readiness platform developed as an Information Technology Institute (ITI Intake 46) 9-Month Professional Graduation Project. It unifies CV ingestion, adaptive mock interviews with multimodal cues, ATS job matching, performance analytics, and competitive challenges.

How is Career Pilot's AI different from generic chatbots?+

Career Pilot grounds all AI questions and evaluations in candidate profile context and curriculum standards using PostgreSQL pgvector semantic retrieval, eliminating hallucinations and ensuring realistic STAR interview scoring.

What native mobile platforms are supported?+

We built two fully native clients: Kotlin + Jetpack Compose on Android (with MediaPipe ML), and Swift + SwiftUI on iOS (with Apple Vision ML).
